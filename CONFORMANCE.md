# Conformance

How provkit 0.1.0 relates to the W3C PROV Recommendations and to ProvPy 3.2.2
(`prov` on PyPI) and to ProvToolbox 2.0.4. Four sections follow, then the
corpus the tests read.

- Departures from the Recommendations: behaviour that differs from the
  grammar or the prose of PROV-N, PROV-DM, PROV-JSON or PROV-CONSTRAINTS, each
  by decision.
- Differences from ProvPy 3.2.2: behaviour where ProvPy differs from the
  grammar and provkit follows the grammar, and behaviour the grammar does not
  decide, where the two libraries differ. Each row cites the issue filed on
  [trungdong/prov](https://github.com/trungdong/prov/issues), or says that one is
  to be filed or that none is needed.
- Differences from ProvToolbox 2.0.4: behaviour a user who moves a document
  between the two tools meets, each from a run against `provconvert` 2.0.4 or
  the services at openprovenance.org (2.2.1).
- Implementation limits: what the library does not do, and choices that
  a caller might not expect.

Each row gives the behaviour, the reason and the test, written as the test
name in backticks and its file in parentheses. Files are under `provkit/` unless noted. A row that cites
`probes_read_as_the_grammar_requires` (tests/corpus_probes.rs) is asserted by
the named probe files in `corpus/provn/probes`, under both profiles.
`expected.tsv` in that directory lists every probe on which ProvPy differs,
with its issue number.

## Corpus

The tests read [prov-corpus](https://github.com/trungdong/prov-corpus) v2.2.0
(submodule at `corpus/`). ProvPy shares it. Failing files are collected and
reported together.

### PROV-JSON

398 documents. All 398 read, round-trip through PROV-JSON and round-trip
through PROV-N.

Tests: `reads_all_398`, `round_trips_all_398` (tests/corpus_json.rs),
`json_corpus_round_trips_through_provn` (tests/corpus_twins.rs).

### PROV-N specification examples

127 examples: 64 from PROV-N and 63 from PROV-DM. 100 parse under `Strict`. The
other 27 are excluded, and each is asserted rejected. The grammar rejects them
or they are not complete PROV-N:

- Not a statement or not complete: `prov-n-example-52`, `-53`, `-54`, `-55`,
  `-56`, `-59`; `prov-dm-example-05`, `-06`, `-19`, `-57`, `-58`, `-59`.
- A required argument missing:
  `prov-n-example-37`; `prov-dm-example-03`, `-04`, `-16`, `-24`, `-34`,
  `-52`, `-53`, `-55`, `-56`, `-63`.
- A statement the grammar orders otherwise: `prov-n-example-51` (`default`
  after `prefix`, production [45]) and `prov-dm-example-42` (a statement after
  a bundle, production [1]).
- `prov-n-example-16`: a time argument that is not a `dateTime`.
- `prov-n-example-61`: a bundle identifier with no default namespace.

The exact list and reasons are in
`provkit/tests/data/excluded_spec_examples.tsv`. ProvPy 3.2.2
excludes 27 too, but a different set. It excludes `prov-n-example-63` and
`-64` (extensibility expressions, #520) and accepts `prov-n-example-51` and
`prov-dm-example-42` under `strict` (#519).

Test: `spec_examples_parse_under_strict` (tests/corpus_provn.rs).

### ProvToolbox files

9 files. 6 parse under `Default`. 3 are excluded, listed with their reasons
in `provkit/tests/data/excluded_provtoolbox_documents.tsv`:

  productions [41] and [43] forbid.

Tests: `provtoolbox_files_parse_under_default`,
